About me

I'm an Environmental Resource Conservationist, GIS and Remote Sensing professional, and increasingly interested in leveraging technology as a tool to address environmental and spatial issues. I hold a degree in Environmental Studies (Resource Conservation), which provided me with a grasp of environmental management, conservation, and peoples relationship with their environment. Throughout the journey, I have always have an interest in technology hence wondering how I would be able to combine these fields. Then boom!! GIS and remote sensing came along.

GIS allowed me to combine environmental knowledge with technology, data, mapping and visualization. Along my professional journey, I have noticed a lack of comprehension and understanding of GIS in Kenya. This reflects the extent to which GIS still works in the background, even though it has all sorts of potential applications for managing the environment, making land use decisions, planning cities, conducting research, doing business, building infrastructure, and making daily decisions.

I see this as an opportunity to present the spatial technology in a more visible, accessible and understandable manner, beyond the context of technical mapping. This is what inspires my work. It is through my projects and initiatives, that I'm showcasing the use of technology to make environmental and spatial information more visible, understood and used by people.
